LEVEL 04 / ASK THE GUIDE

Good to know.

Do I need to know how to code?+

No. Describe what you want in everyday language. Luban generates a script that you can review and try before saving.

Which AI provider can I use?+

Connect your own AI credentials in the extension settings. Luban supports its configured providers and custom OpenAI-compatible endpoints.

Am I in control of what runs?+

Yes. Review and confirm a tool before running it. Manage site permissions and automatic execution, or restore the previous saved version.

Can I take my tools with me?+

Export your tools as standard userscripts to use with a compatible userscript manager.

MAKE YOURSELF AT HOME

Meet your new
browser companion.

Luban is currently installed as an unpacked Chrome extension. Chrome 138 or later is required.

  1. Build the extension from the Luban project:npm install
    npm run build
  2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Choose Load unpacked and select the project's dist folder.
  4. Open Luban settings and connect your AI provider.
